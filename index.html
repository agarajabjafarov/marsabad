Cultural Legacy
Azerbaijan
Was Already There.
1201
Nasraddin Tusi
Founded Maragha Observatory — medieval world's most advanced astronomical center. His tables used for 300 years.
1946
Habibulla Mammadbeyli
Led expeditions in Khizi region to select future observatory site. The science of MARS began on this very soil.
1959
Shamakhi Observatory
Largest astrophysical observatory in the Caucasus. Named after Tusi. 40 km from MARS site.
1982
Ibrahimov Crater · Mars
A crater on Mars named after Azerbaijani astronomer Nadir Ibrahimov. 87 km diameter. Azerbaijan is already on Mars.
1987
Musa Manarov
Born in Baku. First human to spend a full year in space. Total 541 days. World record. Hero of the Soviet Union.
1917
Karim Kerimov
Azerbaijani mastermind of the Soviet Space Program. Headed State Commission for 25 years. Launched Gagarin's era.
2006
Anousheh Ansari
First female space tourist. First Muslim woman in space. Self-funded $20M ISS mission. Global icon.
2013
Azerspace-1
Azerbaijan's first national satellite. Currently operational. Active space infrastructure exists today.
